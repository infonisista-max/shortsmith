# Style: animated
Motion-graphics heavy; illustrated B-roll dominates. DRAFT — complete in grilling.

## Beat grammar
- Beats 2–4 s; presenter mostly PIP or off; hook = animated title card.
## B-roll
- AI-generated or vector illustrations with strong motion (parallax, pop-in, path animation); consistent palette per short.
## Captions
- Kinetic captions that pop per phrase; two-colour emphasis.
## Sound
- Upbeat bed ≈ 10 dB under voice; pops and thumps on pop-ins; no sweeps or risers.
## Finale
- Animated end card.
