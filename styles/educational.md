# Style: educational
Calm, clear teaching. DRAFT — complete in grilling.

## Beat grammar
- Beats 4–8 s; presenter full-frame for setup and summary, PIP during diagrams; hook = the question the video answers.
## B-roll
- Diagrams, labelled images, step cards, simple animations of a process; sources as in explainer.
## Captions
- Full sentences up to 7 words, neutral typography, key term highlighted.
## Sound
- Soft ambient bed ≈ 14 dB under voice; no hits except a soft tick on step changes; no sweeps.
## Finale
- One-line recap card.
