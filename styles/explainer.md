# Style: explainer
Fact/story explainer for Hindi/Hinglish and English audiences. High information density, presenter-led, B-roll on every beat. Default style.

## Beat grammar
- Beats 2–6 s. Hook beat first: presenter full-frame or bold hook card, ≤ 8 words on screen.
- Presenter: full-frame on personal/emotional lines; round or framed PIP while B-roll carries facts; off-screen for archival/map/chart beats. Never more than ~2 consecutive beats in the same mode.
- PIP framing: whole head + neck/collar visible; circle window sized to the frame width; never a tight face crop.

## B-roll
- Kinds: photo with Ken Burns, framed archival card with zoom-to-point and red ring, map with pin, simple chart, stamp/label, lower-third name label, split-screen for comparisons.
- Sources in order: user-supplied images → rights-safe libraries → AI-generated cinematic 9:16 image. Every asset logged with source and licence.

## Captions
- Word-synced, 2–5 word phrases, bold sans (Poppins-class), white with dark outline, one keyword per phrase highlighted; lower third, never under the PIP.

## Sound
- Bed: deep minor-key sub-bass or trap-style beat ≈ 10 dB under voice, light ducking, drop on the main reveal.
- Hits: short bass thumps on stamps/reveals only. Forbidden: whooshes, sweeps, risers, rumble crescendos, chimes, ticks.
- Voice −14 LUFS, −1.5 dBTP.

## Finale
- Payoff line + call-to-action card in the last 3 s.
