# Style: hitech
Dark, glowing, product/tech facts. DRAFT — complete in grilling.

## Beat grammar
- Beats 2–5 s; presenter framed PIP with glow border; hook = spec/number stamp.
## B-roll
- Product images on dark gradient, UI mock cards, spec stamps, number counters; sources as in explainer.
## Captions
- Monospace-flavoured bold, cyan/white emphasis, upper third when B-roll fills the lower half.
## Sound
- Sub-bass electronic bed ≈ 10 dB under voice; single low thump on reveals; no sweeps or risers.
## Finale
- Spec summary card.
