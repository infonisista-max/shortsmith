# 022 — Sound director core: catalogue, bed selection, floor hits, cues, envelope, mix graph, stems

## Type

AFK

## Parent PRD

`issues/prd.md`

## What to build

The music bed and sound hits arrive in the short. The sound director loads the audio catalogue as text, selects the bed by tag overlap and energy distance with drop-point tie-break, derives the Dyson v2 floor hits from plan events, matches planner cue intents to SFX by tag with the floor hit as fallback, applies cue caps and per-class levels, builds the mood-curve envelope within bounds with drops as steps at beat boundaries, and builds the research §5 mix graph verbatim: voice chain, sidechain ducking, master loudnorm and limiter, stems written beside the mix. `FakeAudioSearch` returns seeded catalogue entries only. Tests and smoke use a synthesized test catalogue generated by conftest (tone beds and click SFX), never committed audio; the real seed is ticket 025. Music and SFX rows are appended to the rights log by the renderer.

Covers PRD `sound`, the mix half of `render`, rights rows for audio. Decisions 7.1, 7.2, 7.3, 5.4, 12.1.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `assets/audio/catalog.yaml` schema per 7.2 loads into a Pydantic model; conftest writes a temporary catalogue with synthesized files for tests and smoke.
- [ ] Bed selection: score = tag overlap (theme, mood) + energy distance from `bed_query`, ties by drop-point fit to the first stamp; below the threshold the audio search adapter is called (Freesound in 024; `FakeAudioSearch` here); the chosen bed gets a rights row with origin `library`.
- [ ] Floor hits derived from plan events: bass on stamps, reveals and headers; drum on money reveal and the finale word; thump on card fly-ins; nothing on whip cuts, punch-ins, rings or lower-thirds.
- [ ] Planner cues matched by `intent` tag; unmatched intent → nearest floor hit; `cues_max: 20` per 60 s including floor hits, `cues_per_beat_max: 1`, every cue −2 to −10 dB under voice by class, none louder than −2 dB.
- [ ] Mood envelope on the music stem: swell ≤ +4 dB, drop ≥ −8 dB, ramps ≥ 1.5 s, drops as steps at beat boundaries followed by a changeover cue; bed target −11 dB vs voice RMS while speaking, fades 0.6 s in / 1.2 s out.
- [ ] Mix graph: voice chain from 005, bed sidechain threshold 0.06 ratio 2 attack 20 release 400, ducking ≤ 4 dB, master two-pass loudnorm −14 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP + limiter 0.891; `work/stems/{voice,music,sfx}.wav` always written; a stem balance report (median bed level vs voice, speech band margin) written to `work/stems/balance.json`.
- [ ] Acceptance in code: bed median −9 to −12 dB under voice, speech band 250 Hz–4 kHz ≥ 20 dB above the bed; outside → the mix step fails with the measured numbers.
- [ ] Boundary tests, `tests/test_sound.py`: envelope clipped at +4/−8, ramp under 1.5 s rejected, 21st cue dropped, floor hits from a synthetic plan, bed selection tie-break.
- [ ] Smoke: the fixture short has a bed and at least one floor hit; T4 passes on the full mix.
