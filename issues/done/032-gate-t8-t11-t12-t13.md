## Notes from 032 (done 2026-09-25)

- `technical.report(checks)` passes only when every name in `CHECK_ORDER` is present and `pass`; a report that stopped at a FAIL, or a pre-032 `qa.json` with `not_implemented` rows, never delivers. `PLACEHOLDERS`, `IMPLEMENTED` and `placeholder()` are gone; the `not_implemented` status literal stays only so older reports on disk still load on the job page (grey dot, "not implemented" label).
- T8 needs the specs the grammar judged the plan by, so `technical.run(job, specs=...)`, `TechnicalGate(specs=...)` and the pipeline pass the worker's set through (the smoke's is the fixture-shaped copy; with no specs the gate loads the shipped styles, and a test proves the fixture plan then fails T8 on the real counts). The re-check reads `work/plan.validated.json`, `work/asr.json`, `input/brief.md` and `input/refs.json` (must-use ids as `planning` did); on the fixture the grammar returns the identical plan with zero clamps. `FakeRenderer` now leaves a `work/render.log` like the real one.
- T11 maps each strip frame's face box into the circle (`(x - window_left) * diameter / window_size`, centre at `diameter / 2`) and fails when a corner passes the radius or the chin passes 90 % of the window; a still with no face is counted, not judged (the 3.3 floor already ran). On the fixture the worst corner sits at 123 px of the 150 px radius and the chin at 56 % with the unit test's synthetic box; the real cascade on the smoke measures 57 %.
- T12 follows decision 10.1's element list (captions, stamps, lower-thirds; a counter is a stamp), not "every text element": the hook title is drawn at y 170 (`render.HOOK_TITLE_TOP`, read off nkb_02 / dyson_02) and the finale word at y 1180, both set pieces the reference frames place, so a literal "every text element" rule would fail every short on the reference look. **Operator call:** whether the hook title should move below y 250 (6.3 says nothing readable in the top zone) is a look decision against the reference frames, not the gate's.
- **Latent T12 trip to watch on real jobs:** the caption block is centred with `captions.max_width_px: 960` (6.2), so a line wider than 800 px reaches past x 940 into the right rail (6.3). The fixture's widest line ends at x 844. If a real job fails T12 on a caption word, the fix is a spec decision (narrower `max_width_px`, or a left-anchored block), not a gate change.
- T13 reads the used counts from `work/assets.json` and the allowances from the style's `budget` (judge calls, search queries, generated images), the cash total and per-step totals from `ledger.by_step`, subscription tokens beside them, and the `over_soft_cap` flag as "OVER SOFT CAP (flag only)". It always passes.
- **Decision (HITL, 2026-09-25): per-step cash caps are absent by design, not missing.** The ticket text says "per-step caps"; nothing in the repo defines one. Decision 11.3 gives the ledger three per-job caps (soft, hard, per-day) and CLAUDE.md keeps them unset until the operator derives them from the first ten metered jobs; the style `budget` is count allowances for the asset step only. T13 records exactly what exists: per-step cash totals against no cap, and the count allowances against their ceilings. Do not re-open this as a gap; a per-step cap would need a new decision first.
- The run-level tests build one job through the fake pipeline on the fixture plan (`gated_job`, session-scoped: fake transcriber, planner, sources, renderer, detector under the fixture-shaped spec), then give it the mastered fixture as short and cut and a lifted voice stem, so T8–T13 read what the real steps leave. Each test copies it.
- The reserved-zone numbers live once, in `technical.SAFE_TOP_PX / SAFE_BOTTOM_PX / SAFE_RIGHT_PX`; the contact sheet draws those.
