# 022 — Sound director core: catalogue, bed selection, floor hits, cues, envelope, mix graph, stems

## Type

AFK

## Parent PRD

`issues/prd.md`

## What to build

The music bed and sound hits arrive in the short. The sound director loads the audio catalogue as text, selects the bed by tag overlap and energy distance with drop-point tie-break, derives the Dyson v2 floor hits from plan events, matches planner cue intents to SFX by tag with the floor hit as fallback, applies cue caps and per-class levels, builds the mood-curve envelope within bounds with drops as steps at beat boundaries, and builds the research §5 mix graph verbatim: voice chain, sidechain ducking, master loudnorm and limiter, stems written beside the mix. `FakeAudioSearch` returns seeded catalogue entries only. Tests and smoke use a synthesized test catalogue generated by conftest (tone beds and click SFX), never committed audio; the real seed is ticket 025. Music and SFX rows are appended to the rights log by the renderer.

Covers PRD `sound`, the mix half of `render`, rights rows for audio. Decisions 7.1, 7.2, 7.3, 5.4, 12.1.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `assets/audio/catalog.yaml` schema per 7.2 loads into a Pydantic model; conftest writes a temporary catalogue with synthesized files for tests and smoke.
- [x] Bed selection: score = tag overlap (theme, mood) + energy distance from `bed_query`, ties by drop-point fit to the first stamp; below the threshold the audio search adapter is called (Freesound in 024; `FakeAudioSearch` here); the chosen bed gets a rights row with origin `library`.
- [x] Floor hits derived from plan events: bass on stamps, reveals and headers; drum on money reveal and the finale word; thump on card fly-ins; nothing on whip cuts, punch-ins, rings or lower-thirds.
- [x] Planner cues matched by `intent` tag; unmatched intent → nearest floor hit; `cues_max: 20` per 60 s including floor hits, `cues_per_beat_max: 1`, every cue −2 to −10 dB under voice by class, none louder than −2 dB.
- [x] Mood envelope on the music stem: swell ≤ +4 dB, drop ≥ −8 dB, ramps ≥ 1.5 s, drops as steps at beat boundaries followed by a changeover cue; bed target −11 dB vs voice RMS while speaking, fades 0.6 s in / 1.2 s out.
- [x] Mix graph: voice chain from 005, bed sidechain threshold 0.06 ratio 2 attack 20 release 400, ducking ≤ 4 dB, master two-pass loudnorm −14 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP + limiter 0.891; `work/stems/{voice,music,sfx}.wav` always written; a stem balance report (median bed level vs voice, speech band margin) written to `work/stems/balance.json`.
- [x] Acceptance in code: bed median −9 to −12 dB under voice, speech band 250 Hz–4 kHz ≥ 20 dB above the bed; outside → the mix step fails with the measured numbers.
- [x] Boundary tests, `tests/test_sound.py`: envelope clipped at +4/−8, ramp under 1.5 s rejected, 21st cue dropped, floor hits from a synthetic plan, bed selection tie-break.
- [x] Smoke: the fixture short has a bed and at least one floor hit; T4 passes on the full mix.

## What was built

`src/shortsmith/sound.py` is the whole director; the renderer calls it from `mux`.

- **Catalogue.** `AudioEntry` / `Catalogue` / `BalanceReport` in `contracts.py`; `sound.Library` pairs the parsed entries with the folder their `file` paths are relative to. The shipped `assets/audio/catalog.yaml` is committed **empty** with the 7.2 shape documented in comments — 025 seeds it — and a missing or empty catalogue is not an error: the short is then the voice alone, exactly as before this ticket. `fixture.make_catalogue` synthesises tone beds and click SFX with ffmpeg (never committed, like the clip); `tests/conftest.py` exposes it as the `library` fixture and the smoke builds its own.
- **Where the numbers live.** Everything the style carries is read from `styles.Sound`. The engine's own weights are two small tables beside the code that uses them, like `render`'s geometry constants: `CLASS_LEVEL` (where each hit class sits inside the style's `cue_db_min`–`cue_db_max` band) and `CLASS_RANK` (which cue survives the cap).
- **Floor hits.** `beat_triggers` maps the plan to the event names the style's `sound.floor_hits` keys, most specific first, so a beat that is both a money reveal and a stamp earns the drum. Whip cuts, punch-ins, rings and lower-thirds earn nothing because no style names them — not because code excludes them.
- **Cues.** Planner intents first, then a changeover on every envelope step (7.3 requires a drop to be *followed by* one), then the floor. A beat takes at most `cues_per_beat_max`. Over `cues_max_per_60s` the classed cues are kept (drum and changeover, then bass, then thump, earlier first) and the planner's extras fill what is left — the floor is what keeps a short from being flat, so it is not what gets thinned first.
- **The bed target is a property of the rendered file.** 7.3 puts the bed `bed_db_under_voice` under the voice *on the median*, and the envelope and the fades both move the median away from the flat gain that would hit it, so `_music_stem` renders, measures the median and corrects the gain — the loop `render.master` already uses for loudness.
- **Rights.** The renderer's music and SFX rows go to `work/audio_rights.json` (`rights.write_audio`) and every later `rights.write` appends them, so a re-run of the asset step rebuilds the picture rows and carries the audio ones through (the 016 note). `credits.md` gained `Music:` and `Sound:` lines.
- **The log.** The director's one-line summary (bed, cue count, where the cues came from) is a `job.log` step note, `sound: bed ...`, the shape the asset step already uses. Step notes sit between the status lines, so the smoke and `tests/test_smoke.py` both compare the status trail against the transitions alone and assert the note separately — inside `rendering`, where it is written.
- **Wiring.** The catalogue is loaded once by whoever builds the worker, like the specs, and used twice: `Library.tags()` is the sound call's `## 8` section, and the renderer mixes from it (`Renderer.render(..., library=)`). `pipeline.CATALOGUE_TAGS` is gone.
