# 031 — Technical gate T5, T7 and T10: caption coverage and lip-sync, luma and frozen frames, mid-word cuts

## Type

AFK

## Parent PRD

`issues/prd.md`

## What to build

Three signal-analysis checks in `qa.technical`, run in order after T4 and T6 and before T8. T5 samples three random timestamps and asserts a caption page covers each, then measures lip-sync lag by envelope cross-correlation of the voice stem against the cut clip's audio (0 ± 1 frame). T7 asserts mean luma ≥ 12/255 on every frame and no identical-frame run longer than 0.5 s before the finale, via signalstats and framehash. T10 asserts every `cut` boundary lies within 0.03 s of a word start or end.

Covers PRD `qa.technical` T5, T7, T10. Decisions 10.1, 3.1, 6.1.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] T5: three timestamps drawn from a seeded RNG recorded in `qa.json`; each is inside a caption page's window; lag measured by cross-correlation with numpy, reported in frames, fails outside ±1.
- [x] T7: per-frame mean luma from signalstats, fail on any frame < 12/255 with the time; framehash runs of identical frames > 0.5 s fail with the span, excluding the finale.
- [x] T10: cut boundaries from `work/cut.json` compared to the fixed word list; fail names the boundary and the nearest word.
- [x] Each check has a passing and a failing synthetic input test (a page gap, a shifted voice stem, a black frame inserted, a frozen second, a cut 0.05 s inside a word).
- [x] `qa.json` order is T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, T6, T7, (T8 placeholder), T9, T10, (T11–T13 placeholders); placeholders are recorded as `not_implemented` and do not pass silently: `delivered` requires every listed check to be `pass` once 032 lands, and until then the summary panel shows them grey.
- [x] Smoke passes T5, T7 and T10 on the fixture.

## Done 2026-09-25

- `qa.technical`: `CHECK_ORDER` T1–T13, `IMPLEMENTED` T1–T10, `PLACEHOLDERS` T11–T13. `QaCheck` gained `status: pass | fail | not_implemented` (derived from `passed`; a `not_implemented` check is never `passed`, the model refuses it). `QaReport.failed` is the first `fail`; `technical.report(checks)` passes when nothing failed. `run` stops only on `fail`, so the placeholders are always listed last.
- T5 samples three *words* (seed 101, `random.Random`, from the captioned words on the cut timeline before the finale) and asks for a page at each word's midpoint; the seed and the three times are in the detail. Sampling uniform time instead would land in pauses where no caption is due. Lag: `lipsync_lag_s` cross-correlates 1 ms amplitude envelopes (FFT) within ±0.5 s (the fixture's bursts repeat every second) of `work/stems/voice.wav` against `work/cut.mp4`'s audio; positive is the stem late. On the smoke render the lag is +0 ms: the voice chain and AAC round trip shift nothing.
- T7 reads `ffmpeg.frame_stats`: one decode with `signalstats` (YAVG on the log) and the `framehash` muxer (MD5 per video frame, video stream mapped alone or the audio frames are hashed too). Limited-range Y is mapped to full range (`luma_full`: 16→0, 235→255) before the 12/255 line, else a black frame reads 16 and passes; `color_range=pc` is read raw. A run of n identical frames is n/fps s; 15 frames pass, 16 fail; frames from the finale's start are exempt.
- T10 is the mid-word rule, not "within 0.03 s of an edge": a boundary more than 0.03 s inside a word fails naming the boundary, the word and the distance to its nearest edge; a boundary in a pause passes (the grammar's snap treats silence the same way, and the recording's own edges would otherwise fail). `work/cut.json` (`contracts.CutList`) is written by `render.cut_presenter` and `FakeRenderer` via `presenter.write_cut_list`; T10 and T5 read it with `presenter.load_cut_list`.
- T8 stays the 016 partial (rescue limit), not a placeholder; the rest of T8 is 032's.
- `FakeGate` records the placeholders too; the job page shows them as "not implemented" (class `not_implemented`, muted), the sheet grey; the smoke summary reads "T1 … T10 pass, T11 T12 T13 not implemented".
- Test note: a frozen second in a *lossy* x264 encode is not bit-identical for its first frames (the encoder keeps refining the held picture), so the frozen-clip tests encode losslessly (`-qp 0`) and build the hold with `tpad=stop_mode=clone` (`loop` does not keep CFR timestamps). A real short whose picture freezes will show a run a few frames shorter than the freeze; the 0.5 s line reads the identical stretch.
