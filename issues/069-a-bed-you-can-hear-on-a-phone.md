# 069 — A bed you can hear on a phone: searches ask for music, and a bed the speaker cannot play is refused

## Type

AFK — no new packages.

## Parent PRD

`issues/prd.md`

## What to build

Run04 (job `20260928-140620-f774e1`, vishva). Operator's phone verdict: "I heard no
background music. For a Saudi-king story I expected light Middle-East music, or a low
news-style bed." The job log says a bed was placed (`freesound_557546`), and
`work/stems/balance.json` passed it at −14 dB under the voice. Three faults stacked up:

1. **A library bed can never win on tags.** `bed_score` (`sound/__init__.py:214-221`)
   asks whether the whole query string equals one tag: `"history documentary" in
   {"history", "documentary", …}`. That is false, so the overlap is 0. The planner always
   writes several words (`bed_query: {theme: "history documentary", mood: "regal
   intriguing eastern oud", energy: 3}`). So every library bed scores at most 0 against
   the 0.5 threshold, and the search runs every time. 738836 scored −0.1.
2. **The search ladder narrows to words that are not about music.** The first three rungs
   found 0 hits. The fourth was `'regal'`, and its first hit was a Buick Regal's exhaust
   (see 068). No rung says "music", and the request does not filter to music.
3. **The balance gate cannot hear a bass-only bed.** The car recording is −14.9 dB
   full-band but −27.6 dB above 200 Hz. In `music.ducked.wav`, everything above 200 Hz
   sits at −49 dB against the voice's −19 dB. That is about 30 dB down, which a phone
   speaker does not reproduce. The checks are full-band RMS
   (`bed_accept_db`) and a *minimum* speech-band margin (12 dB). The measured margin of
   28.7 dB was the warning, but nothing bounds it from above.
   - The only other measured job is run03 (`20260927-140915-bbad1c`, 056): margin 9.4 dB.
     The operator heard that bed ("a bit loud").

**Amended 29 Sep 2026 (grill on learning from references).** Fault 1 is **not** fixed
here. 076 replaces the free-text bed query with a closed mood + flavour list
(`assets/audio/moods.yaml`, 073) and selects library beds by those closed tags, so
word-level scoring of free text would be built only to be removed. This ticket keeps the
two faults that stay true on every path: the Freesound fallback must search for music
(fault 2), and no bed the phone cannot play may pass (fault 3). The planner-prompt change
is dropped too (076 rewrites the bed part of the sound prompt).

What to change (numbers in each style's `sound` front matter, never in code):

- **Music-anchored searches.** Every `bed_queries` rung carries the style's new
  `sound.bed_query_anchor` (shipped as `music` in all seven styles). The Freesound bed
  request adds a `tag:music` filter. 068's adoption check is the backstop.
- **Audible on a phone.** A new front-matter number, `sound.speech_band_margin_max_db`,
  sits beside the existing minimum. A bed whose speech-band margin is over it fails the
  7.3 balance and is reported in `balance.json`. As 056 (1) already does for any failed
  bed, the director walks on to the next candidate.
  - The agent ships 20 dB in all seven styles as a starting value between the two
    measured runs (9.4 heard, 28.7 not heard). The done note names it for the operator to
    confirm on run05's phone verdict.
  - The job never fails for this. If no candidate passes, the existing 056 rule applies:
    the short goes out with the best-balanced bed and a job-page notice naming the
    margin.
- **The audibility check is a reusable function**, because 075's shortlist tool
  runs it on every bed candidate before the operator hears it.
